Our History of Ice Cream

One recipe.
Three generations of trust.

Founder Earle Swensen opened the doors at Union and Hyde in 1948. He selected the highest-quality ingredients he could find and kept adjusting his recipes until they were just right.

Richard Campana started working here at fifteen. He went on to make ice cream for more than fifty years, and Earle entrusted him to continue the Swensen's legacy.

Today, Richard is retired and his daughter and son-in-law, Diane and Jim, carry the parlor forward. Jim learned the craft from Richard—the kind of knowledge passed person to person, batch by batch.

The recipe survived because the teaching did.
