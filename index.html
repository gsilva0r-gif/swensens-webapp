Our Story

The parlor that started it all.

Earle Swensen opened this shop in 1948, and it's still the original location of the Swensen's name — a certified San Francisco Legacy Business, scooping beside the Hyde Street cable car line for more than 75 years.

Nothing about how we make ice cream has changed: classic recipes, the best possible ingredients, and every batch churned by hand, on-site, in small quantities.
